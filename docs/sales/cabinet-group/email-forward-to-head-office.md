### What Hello Cara is

Hello Cara is an **AI voice receptionist** for the shop phone. She answers everyday questions — opening hours, parking, click & collect, departments, and similar — using information the store provides.

When she cannot fully help, she **captures the caller’s request** and sends a **clear follow-up to the right department** (bakery, meat counter, management, and so on), with the details already logged — not a vague scribbled message.

Store managers can **review calls**, work through follow-ups, and **teach Cara** new answers over time, a bit like training a new team member on the shop floor.

### Why we think it is worth testing

- **Every call gets an answer** when staff are on the floor or already on another line.
- **Callers are not left hanging** — if a person is needed or a transfer fails, Cara takes a message.
- **She does not guess** — if she is unsure, the request goes to staff as a follow-up rather than made-up information.
- **Orders, enquiries, and complaints** can be routed to the **right department** with structured details (for example, a birthday cake order to Bakery, a butcher pre-order to Meat counter, a serious complaint to Management with an optional SMS alert to the store manager).
- Cara is **not** set up to take **card or payment details**, confirm **live shelf stock from memory**, or discuss **named customer orders** with third parties — those stay with staff.

### Data protection and security (short version)

- The **store / group remains the data controller** for caller data; Hello Cara acts as **data processor** under GDPR.
- Data is stored **primarily in Ireland / the EEA**; call recordings are kept **up to 30 days**, then **automatically deleted**.
- Callers are told they are speaking to an **AI assistant** and that calls **may be recorded** before recording starts. We provide templates for **website and in-store signage**.
- Encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, and audit logging for sensitive actions (including GDPR export and erasure).
- **Article 28 Data Processing Agreement** available before go-live.
