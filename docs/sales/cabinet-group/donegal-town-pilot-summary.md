## What Hello Cara does

- Answers common caller questions (hours, location, parking, click & collect, departments).
- Captures orders, enquiries, and complaints as **structured follow-ups** to the right **department** (Bakery, Meat counter, Management, etc.).
- **Does not** take card payments, confirm live shelf stock from memory, or discuss named customer orders with third parties.

---

## What success looks like

- Calls answered when staff are busy on the shop floor.
- Department tickets are clear and actionable (not vague messages).
- Gareth and the team are comfortable using the dashboard.
- Group has enough evidence to decide: continue, expand, or stop — with no long-term lock-in from the pilot itself.
